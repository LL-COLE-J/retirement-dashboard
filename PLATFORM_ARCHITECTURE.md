# Bastion Platform Vision & Architecture

Locked phase: 2.43 — Bastion Platform Identity Lock  
Locked on: 2026-05-10

## Mission

Bastion is a lifetime financial intelligence platform: **Full life planning. One intelligent system.**

Bastion exists to help people, advisors, and institutions see financial paths before decisions become irreversible. It is not positioned as a retirement calculator, budgeting app, or short-term fintech widget. It is a modular planning ecosystem for any occasion, any age, and any path.

## Core philosophy

- **Any occasion:** Bastion should support meaningful financial decisions before, during, and after major life events.
- **Any age:** Planning should work across early career, family formation, peak earning, transition years, retirement, legacy, and enterprise oversight.
- **Any path:** Scenarios should remain reversible, comparable, and anchored to a stable baseline.

## Enterprise direction

Bastion's institutional direction is advisor-grade and enterprise-ready:

- team oversight
- governance reviews
- audit trails
- permissioned operations
- report-grade outputs
- compliance-aware planning boundaries
- client portal workflows

Enterprise/admin surfaces should evolve separately from consumer planning surfaces and must not compromise the clarity of the main user flow.

## Progressive disclosure strategy

Bastion should reveal complexity in layers:

1. **Decision first:** answer, risk, runway, and next action.
2. **Scenario second:** what changes when the user changes a path.
3. **Assumption third:** inputs, confidence, and missing-data warnings.
4. **Technical detail last:** tax, compliance, report, audit, and enterprise controls.

This keeps Bastion understandable in moments of stress while preserving institutional depth for advisors and teams.

## Long-term ecosystem goals

- Preserve one intelligent planning system instead of disconnected calculators.
- Expand modular planning domains only after ownership boundaries are clear.
- Keep financial formulas engine-owned and auditable.
- Keep critical changes visible, reversible, and attributable.
- Maintain premium, institutional presentation across public, app, enterprise, and report surfaces.
- Prepare for enterprise governance without turning the consumer product into an admin console.
