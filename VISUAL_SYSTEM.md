# Bastion Canonical Visual System

Locked phase: 2.43 — Bastion Platform Identity Lock  
Locked on: 2026-05-10

## Visual philosophy

Bastion's approved visual direction is cinematic institutional, premium enterprise fintech, and Beacon Keep tower-led. The product should feel protective, intelligent, stable, and advisor-grade.

## Primary identity

The approved Beacon Keep tower identity is the primary Bastion visual direction. Canonical assets live in `assets/brand/` and should be used according to the brand asset notes:

- `bastion-tower-lockup.svg` for public landing/header surfaces.
- `bastion-tower.svg` for cinematic hero/marketing tower treatment.
- `bastion-shield-b.svg` for operational app and dashboard surfaces.
- `bastion-b-mark.svg` for mobile, favicon, compact navigation, and small UI marks.
- `bastion-report-mark.svg` for reports, PDF, and export surfaces.
- `favicon.svg` for browser favicon.

## Required visual standard

All future imagery must be:

- premium
- cinematic
- enterprise-grade
- production-quality
- consistent with the Beacon Keep tower direction

Future imagery must not use placeholder illustrations, flat generic vectors, stock-feeling graphics, low-detail icons, temporary imagery, or random inconsistent SVGs.

## Surface hierarchy

1. **Public marketing surfaces:** dark cinematic premium presentation using deep navy, muted gold, off-white, elegant hero spacing, and tower-led imagery.
2. **Operational application surfaces:** refined light institutional workspace with clear hierarchy, restrained brand marks, and operational typography.
3. **Enterprise/admin surfaces:** hybrid executive dark/light dashboards with governance clarity, audit readability, and restrained status language.
4. **Reports:** ultra-clean print-grade layouts with report marks, quiet borders, strong hierarchy, and minimal ornament.

## Color and material standards

- Deep navy is the foundation for trust, protection, and cinematic presentation.
- Muted gold is the premium accent and should be used sparingly for beacon, CTA, and emphasis.
- Off-white and refined light surfaces support operational clarity.
- Glass and soft shadows may be used where they improve depth without reducing readability.
- Avoid bright novelty gradients, gimmicky fintech styling, and ungoverned color additions.

## Typography standards

- Public and hero headlines may use elegant serif styling where available.
- Operational surfaces should prioritize clean, readable typography.
- Labels and status text should be concise, plain-English, and decision-focused.
- Avoid jargon unless the surrounding copy explains why it matters.

## Dashboard framing standards

- Dashboard remains output-only.
- The primary answer, top risk, and next action stay above supporting detail.
- Cards should feel like an executive brief: clear, quiet, evenly spaced, and readable under stress.
- Supporting charts and details should never compete with the decision readout.

## Spacing rhythm standards

- Use generous hero spacing on public surfaces.
- Use consistent card gaps on app surfaces.
- Preserve mobile stacking, readable line lengths, and tap targets.
- Prefer progressive disclosure over dense all-at-once layouts.
- Avoid cramped cards, clipped labels, overlapping text, and chart crowding.

## Navigation hierarchy standards

Long-term platform hierarchy:

- Primary: Dashboard, Plan, Scenarios, Advisor
- Planning: Retirement, Tax, Healthcare, Estate, Insurance, Cash Flow
- Intelligence: Risk, Forecasting, Monte Carlo, Alerts, Optimization
- Operations: Reports, Documents, Notes, Client Portal
- Enterprise: Teams, Governance, Audit, Permissions

Current app routes remain preserved until a future owner-approved navigation phase changes routing.

## Mobile direction

Bastion mobile should continue moving toward:

- sticky top navigation
- contextual lower action surfaces
- progressive disclosure
- enterprise mobile clarity
- readable decision-first cards

Mobile changes must not break current routing, owner isolation, calculations, app shell assumptions, or visible Save State sync.
